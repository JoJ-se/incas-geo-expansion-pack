Finding: the cheapest channel (BigIdea, 4.75%) is idle. Prime, carrying 93% of tracked volume, is priced 0.45pp above it — but Prime's settlement limits are lower and more stable than BigIdea's or any other live channel's. Turkey's traffic is small-ticket by design, and that traffic prioritises low, stable limits over the lowest rate. The premium on Prime is a deliberate fit, not simple inefficiency.
